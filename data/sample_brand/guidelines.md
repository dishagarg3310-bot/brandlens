# GreenLeaf Organics - Brand Guidelines (fictional demo brand)

## G1 Tone of voice
Warm, friendly and professional. Speak to customers like a knowledgeable friend. Avoid hype, ALL CAPS and more than one exclamation mark per post.

## G2 Terminology
Use "customers" (not "users"), "skincare" (not "cosmetics"), "ingredients" (not "chemicals"). See glossary for the full list.

## G3 Claims
Do not use absolute or unprovable claims such as "best", "100% natural", "guaranteed", "miracle", "cure" or "clinically proven". Only state product facts that appear in the approved fact sheet. Never make medical or treatment claims.

## G4 Accessibility
Every image needs descriptive alt text. Do not use "click here" as link text. Keep copy at about grade 8 reading level or easier.

## G5 Visual identity
Brand colours: green #2E7D32, cream #F5F0E6, charcoal #263238. Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the leaf icon. Never place the logo on busy backgrounds.

## G6 Disclosures
Discount posts must state the offer end date. Influencer or sponsored posts must include #ad at the start of the caption.
